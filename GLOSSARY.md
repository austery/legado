# Native Reading

A personal reading experience using community book sources. These terms describe product concepts, not a storage schema.

## Language

**Book**: The work the reader intends to read. Under the agreed policy, matching known title and author identify the same work; missing author information does not establish equivalence.

**Source entry**: A particular book listing supplied by one book source, with its own address, table of contents and text. Several source entries may represent the same book.

**Active source**: The source entry currently selected for reading a book. Discovering another candidate does not change the active source.

**Shelf entry**: A book the reader explicitly keeps for later access, initially ungrouped. Reading a book or caching text does not by itself mean adding it to the shelf.

**Shelf**: The reader's collection of explicitly kept books. Its home shows ungrouped shelf entries directly and grouped entries through named group entries.

**Shelf group**: A named collection of shelf entries, presented as a container directly on the shelf home; groups do not contain other groups. Membership is non-exclusive: a shelf entry may appear in multiple groups while remaining one entry on the shelf.

**Ungrouped shelf entry**: A shelf entry belonging to no group, shown directly on the shelf home. Removing its last group membership makes it ungrouped without ending its shelf membership.

**Removal from a group**: Ending a shelf entry's membership in one group, while preserving its shelf membership, other group memberships and reading record.

**Group deletion**: Removing a named group and its memberships while preserving its books on the shelf and their reading records. Books with no remaining group become ungrouped.

**Removal from the shelf**: Ending the reader's choice to keep a book on the shelf and its group memberships, while retaining its reading record. Reading the book again does not by itself add it back; explicitly adding it again creates an ungrouped shelf entry with the retained reading record.

**Reading position**: A location within a particular chapter and text of a source entry. It is distinct from a visual page number.

**Source switching**: An explicit change of the active source for the current book, with the reading location resolved for the replacement text.
_Avoid_: Using this term for choosing which source to search or browse.

**Behavioral reference**: An observed product interaction used to guide a selected reading journey. It does not establish that every feature or internal design should be copied.
